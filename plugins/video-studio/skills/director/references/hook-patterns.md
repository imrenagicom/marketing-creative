# Hook patterns

A hook earns the next few seconds. Choose a pattern by audience awareness and by what you can honestly show. The examples show the mechanism in both languages; the scriptwriter writes the final words natively.

| Pattern | Mechanism | Fits when | English example | Indonesian example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pain-question` | Name the viewer's problem as a question | The problem is common and felt | "Still losing your best ideas in random chats?" | "Masih sering lupa ide bagus yang nyelip di chat?" |
| `demand-proof` | Show that people asked for this | You have real requests or comments | "So many of you asked how I made yesterday's video." | "Banyak banget yang nanya cara bikin video kemarin." |
| `result-first` | Show the finished outcome, then explain | The result is visual and impressive | "This took four minutes. Here's how." | "Ini cuma butuh empat menit. Gini caranya." |
| `myth-break` | State a common belief and contradict it | The audience holds a wrong default | "More features won't fix your onboarding." | "Fitur banyak nggak bikin onboarding lo beres." |
| `number-promise` | Promise a specific, countable payoff | The content is a list or steps | "Three settings that halve your export time." | "Tiga setting yang bikin export dua kali lebih cepat." |
| `cold-open` | Start mid-action, explain after | Story-led or demo-led videos | (open on the cursor dragging the last block into place) | (sama: langsung di tengah aksi) |
| `pov-greeting` | Talk straight to camera as a person | Creator-style, relationship content | "Okay, real talk about pricing pages." | "Oke, jujur-jujuran soal halaman harga." |
| `contrast-before-after` | Show the bad state, then the fixed one | The change is visible | "Before: twelve tabs. After: one." | "Sebelum: dua belas tab. Sesudah: satu." |

## Rules

- **Honesty.** `demand-proof` needs real demand; `result-first` needs a real result. If you don't have it, choose another pattern and list the gap as a risk.
- **First two seconds.** The hook's picture must work with the sound off: the keyword or the visual payoff appears immediately.
- **Payoff.** Note where in the beats the hook is paid off.
- **Register.** Indonesian hooks follow the character's register; the *lo/gua* examples above are casual and change for friendly or formal registers.
