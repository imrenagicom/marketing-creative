# Craft Cartridge: Diegetic Handheld & Camcorder Vlog (`cartridge-diegetic-handheld-vlog`)

Use this cartridge when directing ultra-realistic consumer camcorder footage (Mini-DV, early-2000s CCD tape, or candid smartphone POV vlogs) with natural spoken dialogue and street/campus immersion.

## 1. Diegetic Camera Physics (The Anti-Cinema Lens)

The camera is a physical object held by a person inside the scene—never an invisible floating Hollywood crane:
- **Optical & Sensor Texture**: Specify consumer Mini-DV / early-2000s CCD camcorder characteristics—subtle tape grain, soft highlight roll-off, realistic auto-exposure shifts when passing colored lanterns or stepping into daylight, brief autofocus hunting when crouching close to a subject (like a street cat), and occasional imperfect zoom adjustments.
- **Wrist-First Panning Law**: When switching from an arm's-length selfie shot to show the crowd or environment, the subject must *first rotate their wrist and arm*, letting the lens follow naturally. Never jump-cut or pan without physical arm mechanics.
- **Camera Handoffs**: If a two-shot embrace happens (e.g., meeting a partner at the end of an alley), explicitly direct a companion in the scene to take the camcorder for that beat, then hand it back for the sign-off selfie.

## 2. Period, Cultural & Environmental Authenticity

Build dense, lived-in local geography rather than generic backdrops:
- **Architectural & Street Detail**: Specify concrete alleyway textures (`gang`), potted plants watered with a plastic dipper (`gayung`), tangled overhead cables, parked motorcycles, plastic stools, hanging laundry, warung stalls, or period-accurate 2003 `angkot` and campus classrooms.
- **Era Discipline**: When setting a period piece (e.g., 2003 university vlog), explicitly forbid modern smartphones, modern cars, 4K clinical sharpness, or fake retro VHS glitch overlays.
- **Background Crowd Autonomy**: Passersby, neighbors, and festival visitors must act independently at varying distances—chatting, laughing, waving back when greeted, or briefly occluding the edge of the frame as they walk past. Never allow background extras to stare in unison or perform synchronized robotic animations.

## 3. Spoken Lip-Sync & Location Soundscape

- **Time-Windowed Conversational Dialogue**: Assign short, natural local phrases to specific 5–8 second windows (e.g., `00–05s: "Keluar dulu, siram bunga bentar."`, `15–21s: "Haiii! Happy Halloween!"`, `25–30s: "Dah dulu ya!"` as the hand reaches toward the lens to stop recording).
- **Creator Tone**: Direct the voice as a warm, candid person talking to friends—never a polished TV announcer or commercial voiceover.
- **100% Location Audio**: Keep only natural environmental sound (footsteps on pavement, distant motorcycles, water splashing, cat meow, crowd chatter, laughter, fabric rustle) with realistic acoustic distance. Ban background music, subtitles, and watermarks.
