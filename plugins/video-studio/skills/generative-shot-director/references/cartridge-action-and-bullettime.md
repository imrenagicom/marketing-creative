# Craft Cartridge: High-Speed Action & Bullet-Time (`cartridge-action-and-bullettime`)

Use this cartridge when directing 30-second high-density cinematic action, futuristic vehicle pursuits, or anime-inspired 3D supernatural combat.

## 1. Multi-Beat 3-to-4-Second Pacing

Structure a 30-second sequence into **8 to 9 distinct beats** (`3–4s` each). Never waste the first 3 seconds on a static title card or slow fade-in:
- **Beat 1 (`00:00–00:03`) — Instant Kinetic Hook**:
  - *Pursuit*: Extreme high-angle skyscraper crane-down descending rapidly into fast aerial tracking behind `@Image3`.
  - *Combat*: Start mid-attack with an extreme close-up of the weapon/staff sweeping across the foreground, whip-panning with the energy slash into lateral tracking.
- **Middle Beats (`00:03–00:18`) — Escalation & Tactical Exchange**:
  - Alternate between exterior scale (side-tracking lane changes, low wheel-level whip-pans, aerial corkscrews, low-altitude terrain weaving with foreground rock parallax) and interior/close-up reaction shots (rack focus between driver and passenger, or illusion countertricks).
- **Climax Beats (`00:18–00:30`) — Transformation / Ultimate Reveal & Cliffhanger or Hero Exit**:
  - Gate ultimate abilities or vehicle flight transformations behind an explicit timestamp lock (e.g., `"Thrusters ignite at 00:14"`, `"Do not fully reveal the giant smoke creature before 00:20"`).
  - End either on an extreme wide hero pull-back or a maximum-instability cliffhanger frame at `00:30` immediately before detonation.

## 2. Bullet-Time Slow-Motion Orbit Mechanics

A signature high-production peak is the **Bullet-Time Sequence** (typically placed at `00:09–00:12` and/or `00:18–00:23` / `00:27–00:30`):
1. **World Deceleration vs. Fluid Camera**:
   - Immediately before impact or as vehicle wheels leave the asphalt, slow environmental movement, dust, stone fragments, exhaust vapor, and emergency lights to a near-freeze while the camera continues moving at full fluidity.
2. **180° to 270° Orbit + Rack Focus**:
   - Execute a smooth orbital camera move (`180°` from rear-quarter to front three-quarter, or `270°` around converging fighters) coupled with an explicit rack focus (e.g., from glowing weapon core → fighter's eyes → narrow collision gap; or through car side windows onto the passenger gripping the seat).
3. **Abrupt Speed & Audio Restoration**:
   - During bullet time, stretch and muffle all engine, thruster, and environmental SFX into a deep resonant hum. As the orbit completes, snap back to `1.0x` speed with a radial shockwave or thruster surge and full-frequency sound restoration.

## 3. Physics, Material & Identity Discipline

- **Anticipation → Acceleration → Impact → Recoil → Recovery**: Every attack or evasive turn must obey physical inertia (cloth trailing behind acceleration, suspension compressing before lift, dust scattering from downward exhaust or shockwaves).
- **Material Specificity**: Distinguish elastic gummy deformation (stretches, compresses, and rebounds to its established silhouette, never liquid) from rigid carved masks/pumpkins (never morphing into human facial muscles) and volumetric smoke monsters (turbulent layered clouds and tendrils around glowing eyes, never solid armor).
- **Strict SFX-Only Rule**: When maximum visceral impact is desired, enforce `NO MUSIC, NO SOUNDTRACK, NO DIALOGUE, NO VOICEOVER` so synchronized physical SFX carry 100% of the energy.
